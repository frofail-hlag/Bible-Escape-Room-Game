Bibel Escape Room V3.2.7 — Teacher Self-Registration & Admin Approval

Adds teacher self-registration with Firebase Email/Password, email verification and a resend-verification action. New registrations remain pending until the existing administrator approves them in the in-app Lehrerverwaltung screen. Admin rights are never granted through registration; the first admin is designated manually in Firestore. Approved teachers receive normal teacher access, not admin access. Updated Firestore rules protect teacher requests and role changes. Existing student, Escape Room and Bibel-Challenge flows are retained.
