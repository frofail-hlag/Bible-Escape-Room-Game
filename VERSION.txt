Bibel Escape Room V3.2.8 — Teacher Data Cleanup (EU Frankfurt)

Adds teacher self-registration with Firebase Email/Password, email verification and a resend-verification action. New registrations remain pending until the existing administrator approves them in the in-app Lehrerverwaltung screen. Admin rights are never granted through registration; the first admin is designated manually in Firestore. Approved teachers receive normal teacher access, not admin access. Updated Firestore rules protect teacher requests and role changes. Admin-only cleanup functions remove orphaned teacher request/profile documents after user deletion or during a manual reconciliation. Existing student, Escape Room and Bibel-Challenge flows are retained.
