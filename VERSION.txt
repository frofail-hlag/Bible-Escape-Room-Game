Bibel Escape Room V3.2.7 — Teacher Self-Registration & Admin Approval

Adds teacher self-registration using Firebase Email/Password, email verification, a pending approval request, and an admin-only Lehrerverwaltung screen. Administrators approve/reject requests; approved teachers receive normal teacher access but not admin rights. Firestore rules secure requests and teacher profiles. Existing teacher, student, Escape Room and Bibel-Challenge flows are retained.
